SOGGY SQUIRREL

Bad Fur Day in Bubble Bottom  ·  an original 3D collectathon parody (desktop)

You are Nutmeg, a sarcastic squirrel tourist. Your cruise ship sank, your fur is soaked, and the only way home is to earn the Golden Ferry Ticket from the mayor of Bubble Bottom — a cheerful underwater town of fruit-houses, bubble-taxis and very grumpy crabs.

Talk to Gulliver the Gull, collect 8 Golden Acorns, tail-slap 5 Grumpy Crabs, help Puffer Pete fix his pump, then face the Jelly King. Context pads (!) work like those old cheeky platformers: stand on one and press E.
